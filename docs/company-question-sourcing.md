# Company question sourcing

Company journeys use the curated entries in `lib/reported-problems.ts`. The older banks remain as migration history; their membership is not evidence of a company interview.

To add an entry, read a public first-person candidate account that identifies the company and the question. Record the report link, title, role/round, date (or explicitly unknown), the matching passage in your own words, and review date. Prefer a direct comment permalink when the evidence is in a comment.

Use **Named problem** only when the candidate explicitly names or links the exercise. Use **Reported variation** when the account supports a related exercise, and explain the difference. Company tags, preparation lists, hearsay, and general practice recommendations do not qualify. A source review checks what the report says, not whether the interview actually occurred. Do not claim independent verification, frequency, or current interview coverage.

Use **Reported task** for original prompts without a numbered exercise; link to the report and label difficulty/time as editorial. The generic Google-tagged list was removed from active journeys. Grow these collections as evidence becomes available, without filling a numerical quota.

Historical solved IDs remain stored when entries are removed. Only active questions contribute to completion counts. Card positions migrate to a retained question or the first unfinished card. Foundations retains its original 32 questions.
